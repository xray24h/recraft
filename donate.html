Donate Your Jewellery

Got jewellery you never wear? Fill in the form below and bring it to the ReCraft workshop. The students will melt it down and turn it into something new, and your initials will be engraved on the finished piece.

1. Fill In

Tell us what you are donating, roughly how much it weighs and what it is made of.

2. Drop Off

Bring it to the workshop on the Dundee campus during opening hours.

3. Recraft

Students make a new piece and engrave your initials on it.
