About ReCraft

ReCraft started as a project for the Jewellery Making course at Dundee and Angus College. Instead of buying new metal for practice, students work with jewellery that has been donated by other students and people from the local area.

The Workshop

The workshop is on the Dundee campus. It has everything the students need to melt, cast, shape and polish metal, and it is supervised by a qualified goldsmith.

The Students

The pieces are made by Jewellery Making students. They get real practice melting metal, working with gold and silver and controlling temperature, and they design every piece themselves.

Recycling

Silver and gold can be melted and reused again and again without losing quality. Reusing old jewellery means less new metal has to be mined.

Safety

All melting and casting is done under the supervision of our mentor, a lecturer and goldsmith, who checks that the students follow the workshop safety rules.
